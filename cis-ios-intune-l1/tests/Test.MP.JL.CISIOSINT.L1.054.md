#### Description

CIS Apple iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 Intune Benchmark, recommendation 4.6 (Level 1).

The test passes when the tenant's Compliance policy settings set **Compliance > Compliance policy settings > Mark devices with no compliance policy assigned as** to **Not compliant**.

#### Remediation action

1. In the [Microsoft Intune admin center](https://intune.microsoft.com), go to **Devices** > **Compliance** > **Compliance policy settings**.
2. Set **Compliance** > **Compliance policy settings** > **Mark devices with no compliance policy assigned as** to **Not compliant**.
